# Getting Started Guide

Welcome to the Yamify getting started guide. This guide will help you create your account, set up your first workspace, and connect to the platform.

## Account Creation

1. Go to [yamify.co](https://yamify.co) and click on **Sign Up**
2. Fill out the form with your email and a secure password
3. Validate your email address by clicking on the confirmation link sent to your address

![Account Creation](/images/account-creation.png)

## Logging Into Your Account

Once your account is created and your email is validated:

1. Go to [yamify.co/signin](https://yamify.co/signin)
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click on **Sign In**

## Creating Your First Workspace

A workspace is a work environment that will contain your Yams and applications:

1. From the dashboard, click on the **+ Create Workspace** button
2. Give your workspace a name (for example "My First Workspace")
3. Choose your workspace settings
4. Click on **Create**

![Workspace Creation](/images/workspace-creation.png)

## Creating Your First Yam

A Yam is an isolated environment where you can deploy your applications:

1. In your workspace, click on **+ Create Yam**
2. Give your Yam a name (for example "my-first-yam")
3. Configure your Yam options
4. Click on **Create**

![Yam Creation](/images/yam-creation.png)

## Accessing the Dashboard

Once logged in, you will be automatically redirected to the Yamify dashboard, where you can manage all your workspaces, Yams, and applications.

## Next Steps

Now that you've created your account and set up your first workspace and Yam, you're ready to [deploy your first application](/deployment).
